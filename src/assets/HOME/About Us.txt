About Us

Al-Hayani Company is a Syrian pharmaceutical importation, marketing, and distribution company established in 1997.

The company is officially registered with the Syrian Ministry of Health under Registration No. 3190 and with the Commercial Registry under Registration No. 13356.

For nearly three decades, Al-Hayani Company has built a strong presence in the Syrian pharmaceutical market. We operate through 13 branches across Syria, supported by an established network of distributors, retail pharmacies, physicians, and other healthcare professionals.

Our services and capabilities include:

* Registration of international pharmaceutical companies and their products with the Syrian Ministry of Health
* Pharmaceutical importation and regulatory coordination
* Nationwide warehousing, distribution, and market access
* Physician, pharmacist, and healthcare-provider engagement
* Product launch, promotion, and market-development support
* Digital marketing and social media campaigns
* Access to the Syrian healthcare market, which includes approximately 20,000 pharmacies and more than 25,000 physicians

We work closely with international pharmaceutical and healthcare companies seeking a knowledgeable, dependable, and long-term commercial partner in Syria. Our local experience, regulatory knowledge, and nationwide distribution capabilities enable us to support our partners throughout every stage—from registration and importation to product launch, promotion, and nationwide distribution.

Our mission is to improve access to high-quality healthcare products in Syria while building transparent, sustainable, and mutually beneficial partnerships with pharmaceutical manufacturers worldwide
